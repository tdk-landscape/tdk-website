Which tool is right for you?

Choose TDK if you…

Don't know Kubernetes and don't want to learn it. Need services running locally in minutes. Want manifest-driven simplicity. Are a full-stack or product developer. Work at a startup without a platform team — and want your next five hires coding on day one, not week three.

Choose Garden if you…

Have Kubernetes expertise in-house. Need production-like environments for integration testing. Employ a dedicated platform team with time to maintain YAML pipelines. TDK complements this setup nicely for the fast local loop, by the way.
